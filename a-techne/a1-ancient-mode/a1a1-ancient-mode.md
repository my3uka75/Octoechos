## Ancient Mode - an Outline for New Musical Mechanics

*by Petar Linta and Maria West*

SUMMARY: **Ancient Mode** is a theoretical work by Djuro Zivkovic, published in the form of a white paper. It is presented as a self-sustained **"mode-of-operation"** that governs every aspect of musical composition, including melody, voice-leading, harmony, and tone color. The system is built upon **four deterministic modes of operation**: (a) the mandatory use of the 12-tone chromatic scale as input, (b) the "melodization" of the output scale to ensure directionality, and strict restrictions on motion consisting only of (c) **stepwise motions (+/- 1)** and (d) **leaps (+/- 5)**. A central highlight of the system is the **Matrix**, a polygonal grid where musical navigation follows "rook" moves in chess—horizontal for steps and vertical for leaps. The system also introduces the **Pivot Note**, a single input source from which complex chordal and polyphonic structures are algorithmically derived through a chain of **transformers**. Advanced highlights include **Layer Polyphony (or hyperpolyphony)**, where multiple independent voices are generated from a single pivot note, and **Chordal Vector Interpolation**, which allows for the smooth morphing of chords through expansion or compression.
## Artist's "Manifesto"

The text distinguishes itself through its **synthesis of theological philosophy and high-tech music composition.** It is conceived almost as a manifesto of the **"New Musical Mechanics,"** uniting all dispersed musical elements "into the one" (including atonality, tonality, modality, harmony, counterpoint, and melody, but also strict voice-leading and improvisation). Ancient Mode is **the result of reverse-engineering existing music**, rather than being constructed theoretically without first being grounded in actual sound experience. As Zivkovic understands music primarily as a spiritual dimension of the human condition, he concludes that **Ancient Mode must be the result of,** by this reverse-engineering, **such metaphysical, sacred, and divine energies.** Zivkovic views music as a **"spiritual ascension"** and asserts that meta-music wisdom is a reality where **"faith triumphs over reason."**

Scientifically, the enneatonic **Ancient Scale**[^1] (which is not mandatory for the function of Ancient Mode by default) is unique for having the **lowest difference-tone integrity** compared to other common scales, which generates a vibrant, "phenomenologically rich" acoustic effect that refers to pitches outside itself.

Furthermore, the system’s visualization is novel; it moves beyond traditional 2D pitch-class circles to a **poly-dimensional state** where pitches move through **3D helices**. The text also proposes **new instrument interfaces**, such as utilizing 8x8 MIDI pads to physically navigate the Matrix in real-time, effectively turning the act of composing into a **geometric improvisation**. Finally, the concept of the **Icon-theme**---a self-sufficient, archetypal melody discovered through the system’s internal mechanics---treats musical themes as symbolic objects analogous to sacred icons.
## Elements of the System

- **Mechanical Precision and Identity:** The Ancient Mode defines style as the creation of **"endless possibilities within an extremely limited framework,"** arguing that greater constraints lead to a purer, more crystallized artistic identity.
- **Acoustic Verification:** The framework is built on two pillars: a strict system of limitations and a **definition of error** through acoustic verification, ensuring the music possesses a clear and perceivable identity.
- **Harmonic Stability:** By constructing chords through **stacked leaps**, the system ensures **parsimonious voice-leading** and stable harmonic progressions that remain coherent even within complex textures.
- **Versatility:** Despite its strictness, the Ancient Mode is **"stylistically anaemic,"** meaning it does not dictate a specific aesthetic; it can be used to generate everything from Baroque-like counterpoint to dense cluster-based modernism.
- **Integration of Conflict:** A major strength is its ability to **unify conflicting 20th-century elements**, such as total chromaticism, total diatonicism, free improvisation, and strict voice-leading principles.
## Further Description and Features

There are several features that represent the core of the **theoretical innovation** within Ancient Mode compositional system. One of the most distinctive aspects is the **conceptual separation between pitch-class integers and sounding pitches**, where the two may or may not correspond. For example, a single sounding pitch like D can correspond to different pitch-class integers depending on whether it was triggered by PC2 or PC3 in the chromatic input.

Furthermore, the system rejects the traditional view of a scale as a simple stepwise ladder, instead utilizing the **Second Mode of Operation: Melodization**. This process ensures directed movement by substituting missing pitches with specific patterns—such as the **penultimate note**—to create a semi-ascending scale, or "scalar *cantus firmus*," characterized by **"wave-like contours"**. This results in a scale that functions as a **permanent melodic pattern** or library of snippets rather than a static pitch collection.

The system also provides a **plausible identity for repeated pitches**, treating them as distinct scalar degrees based on their input context. In the melodic genus, two occurrences of the same output note (such as C#) are understood as **separate pitch classes** (e.g., PC1 and PC3) because they possess different potential for motion within the restricted protocols of the system (RLUD-motions).

Finally, the **Matrix** is the essential tool for mapping these pitches and pitch classes into unique positions within a **polygonal grid**. It transforms abstract pitch classes into **precise coordinates** where horizontal rows (RL) represent stepwise motion and vertical columns (UD) represent leaps, effectively acting as a **super-contextual algorithm** for navigating the system's "internal motions".

### Improvisational in its Nature

A further feature that distinguishes Ancient Mode from many contemporary compositional techniques is its ability to allow the composer to **create directly in vivo through improvisation**. This dual nature, combining theoretical sophistication and precision with practical spontaneity, has a significant creative advantage. In Ancient Mode, advanced structural relationships remain fully accessible through a pad-type instrument that represents the Matrix itself.
