### Djuro Zivkovic

#### **Professional Appointments**

- Teacher of Composition, Royal College of Music, Stockholm (https://kmh.se)
- Professor of Composition, Art University, Novi Sad (https://akademija.uns.ac.rs/)
- Permanent member (ledamot) of the Swedish Royal Academy of Music (https://www.musikaliskaakademien.se)

---
#### **Awards & Honors**

- Winner of the *Grawemeyer Award* in Music Composition (2014), the world’s largest composer award.
- Recipient of the Swedish *Grammy Award* (Grammis, 2010) with Sonanza Ensemble.
- Awarded the *Mokranjac Award* (2012) for best Serbian composition.
- *Publisher’s Prize* for “Citadel of Love” (2021 & 2022).

---
#### **Commissions & Performances**

- Works commissioned, performed, and recorded by leading orchestras and ensembles, including: New York Philharmonic, Chicago Symphony Orchestra, Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra, BBC Symphony Orchestra, Klangforum Wien, ASKO|Schönberg Ensemble, Ensemble Modern, Swedish Radio Choir, Sydney Symphony Orchestra, Swedish Chamber Orchestra, Seattle Symphony Orchestra, Zagreb Philharmonic, BIT20, L’Itinéraire, Avanti!, Netherlands Radio Choir, etc.
- Collaborations with renowned conductors and soloists including: Baldur Brönnimann, Brett Dean, Clement Power, Brad Lubman, Shi-Yeon Sung, Martyn Brabbins, Peter Eötvös, Anna Larsson, Christian Karlsen, Emilio Pomarico, Kaspars Putnins, Pekka Kuusisto, etc.

---
#### **Artistic Focus & Techniques**

- Compositional lineage aligned with Bach, Gubaidulina, Messiaen, Saariaho and Takemitsu
- Artistic inspiration rooted in faith, mysticism, and Byzantine music (but not as a copy)
- Central themes: spirituality, meditation, transcendence, and mysticism
- Developed innovative compositional techniques:
    - Developed innovative techniques: polyrhythmic structures, improvisational frameworks, specialized scales, layered polyphony, heterophonic textures; based on ancient music languages, improvisation and algorithmic composition (MaxMSP, Python, Ableton)
    - Creator of the “Harmonic Field” technique (researched at University of Music, Graz)
    - Developed the “Ancient Mode” a unique and all-encompassing compositional technique

---
#### **Selected Major Works**

“On the Guarding of the Heart,” “Le Cimetiere Marin,” cello concerto “Unceasing Prayers,” “The White Angel,” oratorio “The Mystical Sacrifice,” liturgical drama-opera “Bogoluchie,” violin concerto “Chandangel,” choral works such as five motets “Lyra Laudis,” “Credo.”

---
#### **Critical Acclaim**

- Music described as “captivating and highly concentrated,” “unnatural, echoing beauty,” and “coloured by mysticism and phantasy” (Grawemeyer Award / Dagens Nyheter)
- Recognized for originality, precise notation, and a unique, personal tone language

---
#### **Teaching Philosophy**

- Emphasizes the integration of artistic individuality, technical mastery, and creative exploration in composition.
- Encourages students to draw inspiration from diverse musical traditions, own roots, and personal experience, fostering authentic artistic voices.
- Promotes rigorous study of compositional techniques, including polyphony, orchestration, algorithmic composition, set theory, and innovative harmonic frameworks.
- Supports a holistic approach to music education, combining analytical skills, practical musicianship, and critical reflection.
- Values open dialogue, mentorship, and the cultivation of curiosity, aiming to inspire students to engage deeply with both historical and contemporary musical contexts.
- Advocates for the role of music as a medium for spiritual and intellectual exploration, guiding students to create works that are both personally meaningful and universally important.
